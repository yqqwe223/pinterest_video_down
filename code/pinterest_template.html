📥 Step-by-step guide (3 easy steps)

Step 1: Copy the Pinterest Pin link

  1. Open Pinterest (web or app)
  2. Find the public Pin (video or image) you want
  3. Tap the “Share” icon → choose “Copy link”

    Short link:
